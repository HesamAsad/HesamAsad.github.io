I am a PhD student at The University of Melbourne, working on distributional robustness in vision-language models, supervised by Prof. Christopher Leckie and Dr. Sarah Erfani.

My thesis studies the theory and scalable methods for out-of-distribution generalisation in contrastive learning and foundation vision-language models. More broadly, I work on trustworthy machine learning: distributional and adversarial robustness, deep generative models, self-supervised learning and computational biology.
